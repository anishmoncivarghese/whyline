# whyline 0.3.22

Brainstorm roadmap item 3: decisions can be replaced or withdrawn, reviews
can record their evidence, and there's a command to list and search
decisions.

## What's new

- **`whyline note … --supersedes ID`** records that a new decision replaces
  an old one (repeatable; any unique id prefix). The old decision stays in
  the record as history.
- **`whyline retract ID --because "…"`** withdraws a decision that turned
  out wrong, with no replacement. It adds a visible
  "Retracted: <decision>" entry to `decisions.md`.
- **Only standing decisions reach agents.** `brief` and `sync` no longer
  hand superseded or retracted decisions to the next agent as current
  reasoning.
- **`explain` gets sharper.** When several decisions match a commit but
  only one still stands, that one is the answer at "high" confidence
  instead of an ambiguous "medium". A line whose only decision was later
  superseded or retracted still shows it (it's why the line was written),
  labelled with its status.
- **Review evidence on `note`:** `--verdict approved`,
  `--reviewed-commit SHA`, and `--test "command: result"` (repeatable),
  written as visible lines in `decisions.md` and shown in briefs.
- **`whyline decisions`**:
  - `list` (the default): standing decisions, newest first; `--task`,
    `--file`, `--limit`, `--json`, and `--all` to include superseded and
    retracted ones with their status.
  - `search TEXT`: case-insensitive over the decision, reason, rejected
    options, files and task.
  - `show ID`: one decision in full, including status, binding and review
    evidence.
  Named `decisions` rather than `log`, which would collide with `git log`
  and `whyline timeline`.

## How it was checked

- 15 new tests in `tests/test_lifecycle.py`: supersede and retract (and
  reading both back from a clone), a retracted replacement no longer
  retiring what it replaced, brief/sync exclusion, explain choosing the
  standing decision and labelling a superseded one, review evidence round
  trip, and every `decisions` subcommand and filter.
- Run on this repository's 210 decisions: list, search and show work, and
  this release's decisions are attached to its commit.
