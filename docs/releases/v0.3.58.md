# whyline 0.3.58

Job search, part 3: job-alert emails join your daily shortlist, companies with
only a careers page can be watched, and a job in your tracker can get a drafted
application and interview prep. Nothing is ever sent or submitted for you.

## What's changed

- **Job-alert emails.** Alerts from LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor or any sender
  you allow arrive through a Mail rule and join the next daily fetch. Senders
  are off until you turn them on in the **Alerts** section of the Jobs screen,
  and a message is read only if its sender passes your email provider's
  authentication check. `whyline jobs alert <file>` reads a saved alert email
  and runs no AI; `whyline jobs alerts mail-script` installs the Mail rule
  script (also **Mail rule…** in the console). whyline never visits LinkedIn
  or the other sites behind those alerts.
- **Full descriptions for alert jobs.** When an alert names a company you
  already watch, whyline finds the same job on that company's public board and
  uses its full description. Otherwise the agent gets the alert summary, marked
  as such.
- **Careers pages.** Companies with only a careers page are read through the
  schema.org JobPosting data many sites publish for search engines. whyline
  respects robots.txt, stays on the company's own site, sends no cookies, reads
  at most 20 job pages at one request per second, and says plainly when a page
  needs a browser.
- **Draft application.** `whyline jobs draft <job>`, or **Draft application**
  on a Jobs row, writes a tailored CV summary and a cover letter (under 350
  words) as Markdown and .docx in `~/.whyline/jobs/applications/`. A second
  provider reviews the draft and the first revises it. Keywords your CV can't
  support are listed as gaps, never added.
- **Interview prep.** For a job in `interview` status, `whyline jobs interview
  <job>` or **Interview prep** writes likely questions with STAR answers drawn
  only from your CV, questions to ask, and gaps to prepare.
- **Job search guide.** `docs/job-search.md` covers setup from the console,
  where your data lives and what goes to the AI provider, email and Telegram,
  the sources used, the Mac sleeping, and how to remove all job data.

## Upgrading

```bash
uv tool install whyline@latest --force --refresh
```

Then restart `whyline console`, open **Agents**, choose **Jobs**, and run the
setup check.
