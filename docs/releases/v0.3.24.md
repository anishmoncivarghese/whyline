# whyline 0.3.24

Brainstorm roadmap item 5: decisions follow files through renames, and
`explain` can cover a whole change.

## What's new

- **Renames no longer orphan decisions.** Decisions record paths as they
  were. `explain`, and `--file` in `brief`, `sync` and `decisions list`,
  now also match every earlier name of the file (`git log --follow`).
  When a decision matched only through an old name, `explain` says so:
  "(recorded as old/name.py)", and `--json` carries `matched_path`.
- **`whyline explain --diff`** (working tree) and **`--staged`** (index)
  explain every changed line against HEAD. Each changed line that existed
  in HEAD is blamed as it was there and resolved with the same rules as
  `explain`; purely new lines are counted. Output lists the decisions the
  change touches (with confidence, status and `file:line` ranges), the lines
  agents touched without recording reasoning, the lines with no reasoning
  at all, and a coverage line:
  `Coverage: 1 high · 4 medium · 13 low · 0 unexplained · 191 new`.
  `--json` for tools; deleted files count their removed lines.

## How it was checked

- 15 new tests in `tests/test_renames_and_diff.py`: historical names across
  two renames; explain, brief, sync and decisions finding old-name
  decisions; range blame matching per-line blame (also at a revision); the
  refactored per-line explain matching `explain`; `--diff` grouping and
  coverage, `--staged` vs working tree, no changes, a deleted file, JSON,
  and argument checks.
- Run on this release's own uncommitted change: 6 files, 18 changed lines
  resolved (1 high, 4 medium, 13 low), 191 new, in 1.5 s.
