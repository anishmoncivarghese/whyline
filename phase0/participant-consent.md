# Participant Consent and Data Handling

Read this before the session:

> We are evaluating a workflow for handing coding tasks between AI agents and retrieving the reasoning behind code changes. We are testing the workflow, not you. The session takes approximately 60 to 75 minutes. Participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time.
>
> We will record timings, task outcomes, counts such as clarification or copy/paste events, CLI versions, usage numbers exposed by the CLI, and anonymized notes. Sessions are not audio- or video-recorded by default. We will not place your name, contact information, employer, repository name, credentials, source code, raw prompts, or full agent transcripts in the project repository. Please do not use confidential code or include secrets.
>
> Your results will be identified only by a study alias. Findings may be summarized in project planning or documentation. You may ask us to remove your session's study data before aggregated results are published.

Record verbal or written confirmation as `consent_confirmed=true`. Do not begin without it.
