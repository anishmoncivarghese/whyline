## Review findings

### Strengths

- Within-subject counterbalancing is appropriate for a small directional sample.
- Success thresholds are fixed before data collection and require behavioral evidence.
- Token results are not treated as zero when a CLI does not expose them.
- The provenance stimulus includes confidence and a reason not to trust history blindly.
- Data collection excludes names, source, transcripts, secrets, and employer identifiers from the repository.
- Fixture materialization prevents accidental exposure of the other experimental conditions and facilitator ground truth.

### Blocking before measured sessions

1. Run timed cold, human-brief, and structured-brief pilot trials; automated solvability is already verified.
2. Have a second reviewer execute the protocol from the documents and record ambiguous instructions.
3. Recruit and schedule qualified participants or document the fallback recruitment channel.
4. ~~Decide where contact information and any session recordings are stored.~~ Resolved: contact details stay in an owner-controlled scheduling system outside the repository; sessions are not recorded by default.

### Non-blocking cautions

- Ten participants supports directional decisions, not statistical claims.
- The 150-word human brief cap may advantage structured briefs if users naturally write longer; record whether participants object and run a sensitivity note.
- Product naming is intentionally neutral in the prototype because “AgentDock” remains blocked.

## Security and privacy review

- Synthetic repositories are the default.
- Consent is required and withdrawal is supported.
- Raw prompts/transcripts and contact details are excluded from version control.
- Live adapter probes must not enumerate environment variables or credential stores.

## Decision

**Adjust.** The protocol and fixtures are ready for timed pilot trials, but not for measured participant sessions until all four blocking items are closed.

Two Codex engineering dry runs found and corrected a condition asymmetry: the verification command now appears in every task rather than only in handoff briefs. The dry runs are excluded from product results because local event timestamps were not captured and the conditions were not equivalent at execution time.

Measurement policy v1.0 now fixes timing, token accounting, and data-retention rules before valid pilot collection. Vendor token totals remain reported, but time, correctness, and critical-context misses drive the Phase 0 decision when fixed system context dominates usage.

The timestamped Codex pilot harness now has offline tests for successful JSONL timing/usage collection and termination of a silent process at its deadline; both pass.
