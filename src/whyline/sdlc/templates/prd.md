Fill every section. Write "Not applicable — <reason>" rather than dropping one. No filler.

## Summary
Two or three sentences: what this product or change is and who it is for.

## Problem
The problem being solved, and what happens today without a solution.

## Users and the moment they need this
Who the users are and the situation in which they reach for this.

## Goals
What must be true when this succeeds.

## Non-goals
What this deliberately does not try to do.

## Anti-goals
What would make this a failure even if it works.

## Requirements
Numbered R1, R2… so later documents can refer to them. One testable statement each.

## Success measures
How we will know it worked, with a number or an observable result.

## Risks and open questions
What could go wrong, and what is still undecided.
