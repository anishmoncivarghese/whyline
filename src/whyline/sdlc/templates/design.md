Fill every section. Write "Not applicable — <reason>" rather than dropping one. No filler.

## Summary
Two or three sentences: what is being built and why.

## Context and constraints
What already exists, and the limits this design must live within.

## Requirements covered
The PRD requirements this design delivers, by R-number.

## Approach
How it works, in the order a reader needs to follow it.

## Architecture
The parts and how they connect, with a diagram in Mermaid or ASCII.

## Components and files
Each component or file that is added or changed, and what it is for.

## Data model
Entities, fields, relationships and where they are stored.

## Data and formats
Stored files, wire formats and their versions.

## Interfaces (CLI, console, files)
What a user or another program sees: commands, screens, files.

## Security and privacy
Who can do what, what data is sensitive, and how it is protected.

## Performance
Expected load and size, and the limits that matter.

## Errors and edge cases
What can fail or be unusual, and what happens in each case.

## Testing approach
What is tested, how, and what is deliberately not tested.

## Migration and rollback
How existing data and users move over, and how to undo it.

## Decision records
One entry per decision, ADR-style: decision, context, options considered, consequences.

## Rejected alternatives
Options considered and why they were not chosen.

## Open questions for the architect
What needs a senior decision before or during the build.
